# Trax OS Branding Guidelines

## Official identity

- Product: **Trax OS**
- Tagline: **Your Travel OS**
- Assistant: **Atlas**
- Official owner/publisher: **Marcel Marinus Bijl, trading as aXeTech**
- Official website: [trax-os.io](https://trax-os.io)
- Official GitHub organisation: [aXeTech-NL](https://github.com/aXeTech-NL)

## Official builds

Only builds signed and distributed by aXeTech may be presented as official Trax OS builds.

Official distribution channels may include:

- Apple App Store;
- Google Play;
- GitHub Releases;
- GitHub Container Registry;
- the official Trax OS website;
- approved desktop app stores.

Official signing keys, package identifiers, store accounts and release credentials are not part of the open-source licence.

## Fork branding

A fork or materially modified distribution must:

- use a distinct product name;
- use a distinct logo and app icon;
- use its own package, bundle and application identifiers;
- use its own signing keys;
- use its own update infrastructure;
- identify its own publisher and support contact;
- avoid suggesting official endorsement.

It may include a secondary attribution such as:

> Based on Trax OS.

## White-label deployments

The public product may expose portable workspace-branding and custom-domain configuration. Using those capabilities does not grant a licence to present a deployment as an official Trax OS service.

A white-label deployment should use the agency's own customer-facing identity, legal links, support details and domain. A separately distributed branded application also requires distinct package identifiers, signing keys, publisher accounts, update channels and release ownership. Any use of official Trax OS or Atlas marks remains subject to the trademark policy or a separate written agreement.

## Visual system

The detailed Trax OS visual identity is documented in [`docs/design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`](docs/design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md).

The source-code licence does not automatically grant rights to reuse the official logo, app icon, marketing artwork or protected brand assets in a competing or modified product.

## Screenshots and documentation

Screenshots of official builds may be used for:

- reviews;
- tutorials;
- documentation;
- education;
- news reporting;

provided that the context is accurate and does not imply endorsement.

## Integrations

Third-party integrations should use descriptive wording such as:

- “Integration for Trax OS”;
- “Compatible with Trax OS”;
- “Connects to the Trax OS API”.

Do not use wording such as “Official Trax OS Integration” unless written approval has been granted.
