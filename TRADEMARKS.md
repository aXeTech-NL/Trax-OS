# Trax OS Trademark Policy

_Last updated: 25 July 2026_

## 1. Purpose

Trax OS is open-source software. The Apache License 2.0 grants broad rights to use, modify and redistribute the software, but it does not grant a general licence to use the Trax OS or Atlas trademarks.

This policy explains how the following marks may be used:

- Trax OS;
- Atlas, when used for the Trax OS assistant;
- the Trax OS logo;
- the Atlas visual identity;
- official app icons, badges and product artwork;
- confusingly similar names, logos or presentation.

The marks are owned or claimed by Marcel Marinus Bijl, trading as aXeTech, a sole proprietorship registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KVK) (“aXeTech”).

## 2. Uses permitted without separate approval

You may, provided your use is accurate and does not imply sponsorship or endorsement:

- refer to the open-source project by its name, “Trax OS”;
- state that software is based on, compatible with or derived from Trax OS;
- link to the official project repository or website;
- discuss, review, teach or report on Trax OS;
- reproduce the word mark in an attribution or licence notice;
- use screenshots of an unmodified official build for documentation, reviews or tutorials;
- describe an integration as “for Trax OS” or “compatible with Trax OS”.

Examples:

- “Voyago is based on the Trax OS open-source project.”
- “Acme Calendar Connector for Trax OS.”
- “Compatible with Trax OS API v1.”

## 3. Uses requiring prior written permission

You must obtain permission before you:

- call a modified distribution “Trax OS” without a clear distinguishing label;
- offer a hosted service named “Trax OS Cloud”, “Trax OS Hosting” or a confusingly similar name;
- use an official logo or app icon as the primary identity of a fork;
- publish a modified app under official Trax OS package identifiers;
- suggest that a third-party build, fork, service or integration is official, certified, sponsored or endorsed;
- register domains, company names, social accounts or app-store listings that create confusion with the official project;
- use the marks on merchandise or paid promotional material;
- alter the logo and continue to present it as an official Trax OS mark.

## 4. Forks and modified distributions

A materially modified distribution must use a distinct product name and visual identity.

It may state:

> Based on the Trax OS open-source project.

It must not state or imply:

> Official Trax OS

unless it has written authorisation from aXeTech.

A fork should also change, where applicable:

- product name;
- logo and app icon;
- package and bundle identifiers;
- update endpoints;
- cloud endpoints;
- signing identities;
- support contact details;
- privacy policy and terms.

## 5. Unmodified community builds

An exact, reproducible build of an official release may be described as an “unmodified Trax OS community build”, provided that:

- no source changes are made;
- the release version is clearly stated;
- the publisher is clearly identified;
- the publisher does not imply official support;
- the build does not use official signing keys or store identities.

## 6. Domains and app stores

The use of “Trax OS” in a domain, package identifier, app title or publisher name generally requires permission where it may cause users to believe the service or build is official.

Third parties should use their own identifiers, for example:

```text
com.example.voyago
org.community.traxclient
```

rather than official identifiers controlled by aXeTech.

## 7. Logo use

When permission is not required under this policy, the logo must:

- not be distorted;
- not be recoloured in a way that suggests another official product variant;
- not be combined into another organisation’s logo;
- remain secondary to the third party’s own branding;
- include sufficient clear space.

## 8. No effect on copyright licence

This policy regulates trademark and brand use only. It does not reduce rights granted under the Apache License 2.0 for the source code.
